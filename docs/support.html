Common questions

The latest result looks out of date.

Results update automatically after each draw. Pull down to refresh the Home screen. If a new draw still is not showing a while after it happened, email us and we will look into it.

Is this the official Singapore Pools app?

No. Toto Buddy is an independent, educational app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Singapore Pools. It does not accept bets or handle money.

How do I remove my email from the premium waitlist?

Email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete your email and its associated device identifier. If you never joined the waitlist, we hold nothing about you.

Can the app improve my chances of winning?

No, and it never claims to. Every combination is equally likely and the draw has no memory. Toto Buddy exists to make that clear, so you can play informed or decide not to play at all.
